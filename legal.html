Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2025 • Last Updated: June 1, 2025

Study Buddie ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of schools, teachers, students, and parents who use our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website (https://parakk-erp.site) and mobile/web application.

1. Introduction

Study Buddie is designed to support school operations and learning visibility with privacy at the center of every interaction.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  1. a. Account & Registration Data
    • School name, address, and contact details
    • Administrator, teacher, and staff names and email addresses
    • Student names, class/grade, roll numbers, and parent contact information
  2. b. Usage & Learning Data
    • Attendance records
    • Academic performance, grades, and test scores
    • Study time, self-study patterns, and learning engagement data (for Learning Visibility features)
    • App usage logs and interaction data
  3. c. Financial Data
    • Fee payment records and transaction history (processed via secure third-party payment gateways; we do not store full card/bank details)
  4. d. Device & Technical Data
    • IP address, browser type, device type, operating system
    • Cookies and session data for authentication and analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Study Buddie ERP and Learning Visibility platform
  • Generate attendance, performance, and learning analytics reports
  • Send notifications and alerts to parents, teachers, and administrators
  • Process fee payments and generate financial reports
  • Identify at-risk students using predictive analytics (machine learning models)
  • Respond to support queries and improve customer service
  • Comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • School Administrators: Who have authorized access to their institution's data
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors (cloud hosting, payment gateways, SMS/email services) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, court order, or government regulation under Indian law (IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023)
  • We do not share student data with advertisers or unauthorized third parties.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on secure cloud servers (located in India where possible)
  • We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for data in transit
  • Access to sensitive data is role-based and restricted
  • We conduct regular security audits
  • In the event of a data breach, affected users will be notified within 72 hours as required by applicable law

6. Children's Privacy

Study Buddie serves schools that include minor students. We do not directly collect data from children under 13 without verified parental/school consent. All student data is managed through the school administrator account.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for login sessions and non-personal analytics cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

8. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise your rights, contact us at: privacy@studybuddie.in

9. Data Retention

We retain data for the duration of your active subscription plus 2 years, unless a longer period is required by law or you request earlier deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance.
